# Little Break Arcade

A playful, timed mini-game break built into Study Sprint. After completing a focus session, choose **Take a game break** on the progress screen. When the break timer ends, a full-screen reminder offers a direct link to start another study session.

## Games

- **Flappy-ish:** flap through gaps with Space, click, or tap. A bonk gives you a clean new round.
- **Crossy Road:** hop with arrow keys or WASD, or tap the game area.
- **Snack-size Racer:** steer with left/right or A/D, or tap either side of the game.
- **Boop the Hamster:** tap the matching hole or press 1–9 to boop the hamster.

Choose a 5- or 10-minute break from Study Sprint. The countdown begins when **Start my break** is pressed. Game high scores are saved locally in the browser.

## Run and publish

Open this folder's `index.html` directly, or open Study Sprint's `index.html` and choose **Take a game break** on its final progress screen. To publish the combined app, keep this folder inside the Study Sprint repository and publish the whole repository.

## Built with

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Canvas.
